{% else %} {# ── Why recall matters ─────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
Why recall matters more than accuracy for churn. Missing a churner (false negative) means losing that customer's lifetime value — a costly miss. Flagging a loyal customer as at-risk (false positive) only costs a cheap retention offer. With a ~27% churn rate, a naïve model predicting "No churn" always would reach 73% accuracy but catch zero churners. Recall and F1 are the metrics that actually count here.
